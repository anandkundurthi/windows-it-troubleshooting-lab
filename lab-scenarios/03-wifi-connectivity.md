# Lab Scenario 03: Wi-Fi Connectivity

**Type:** Practice scenario. Do not disrupt a production or shared network.

## Scenario
A fictional employee says the computer is connected to Wi-Fi but cannot access a website.

## Objectives
- Determine whether Wi-Fi is disconnected or connected without access.
- Establish whether other sites or devices are affected.
- Inspect IP configuration.
- Use a permitted DNS or connectivity test.
- Record evidence and escalation rationale.

## Suggested commands

```cmd
ipconfig
nslookup example.com
ping example.com
```

Ping may be blocked, so a failed ping alone does not prove that internet access is unavailable.
