# Lab Scenario 06: Printer Not Responding

**Type:** Practice scenario or tabletop exercise. Do not change shared printer settings without authorization.

## Scenario
A fictional employee reports that a print job remains queued and the printer does not produce the document.

## Objectives
- Determine whether the printer is local or shared/network-connected.
- Check power, status, selected printer and whether other users are affected.
- Record any status message.
- Assess impact before changing a shared queue or driver.
